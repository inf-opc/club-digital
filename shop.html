What to buy so a paper game night actually happens.

This list is for one job: 4–12 adults, 60–75 minutes, no app, in a normal apartment. It is not a generic “spooky décor” dump. Each link is a US Amazon.com search so you can pick a seller that still ships before the 31st.

1. A kitchen timer you can hear from the other room

Forbidden Words and The Last Line collapse if someone is watching a phone clock. A cheap magnetic or stand-up kitchen timer keeps the host in the game. Skip smart speakers: they pull the room back onto screens.

Search kitchen timers on Amazon.com

2. Card stock, not copy paper

Print-and-play cards on 80 gsm curl, show the answers through the back, and die after one drink spill. 65–80 lb / 176–216 gsm white card stock is the whole upgrade. One pack covers an After-Dark Club print plus spare bingo sheets.

Search card stock on Amazon.com

3. Caution tape — the one décor that does work for this night

You do not need a hanging skeleton. You need a door that says the living room is now the club. Construction-style yellow tape is what US hosts actually rebuy in October (thousand-plus weekly units on the long rolls). Hang it at handle height, not across a stair.

Search Halloween caution tape on Amazon.com

4. Battery string lights, not a new lamp

Overhead LEDs kill paper games: people cannot read cards and everyone looks washed-out. A warm battery string on a shelf is enough. Avoid mains-powered outdoor strings indoors if you do not have a spare outlet near the table.

Search battery string lights on Amazon.com

5. A physical slap-game as the 10-minute reset

If the writing round flops, you need a thing that does not need rules explained twice. Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza is the current high-volume party card game in this price band (multi-thousand monthly units). It is not Halloween-themed. That is the point: it saves the evening when the theme gets tired.

Search Taco Cat Goat Cheese Pizza on Amazon.com

6. Candy that is not a bowl of one flavour

A variety pack is the snack that doubles as a tie-break prize. Skip chocolate-only if the room is warm; skip bulk hard candy if you have mixed ages in the same flat. NRF still puts candy in the top three Halloween spends — hosts already expect to buy it.

Search candy variety packs on Amazon.com

7. Plastic stemware and a black tablecloth

Real glasses plus paper cards is how the night ends early. Disposable stemware and one cheap black cloth make the table look finished in two minutes and survive red drinks. Buy both only if you do not already own a dark sheet.
