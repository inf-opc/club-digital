What belongs in a bag for two nights, and what does not.

Written for a carry-on and a washing machine at the other end. Not a gear wall. If you already own the thing, do not buy a second one because it is on a list.

1. Two packing cubes, not a new suitcase

One cube for clothes, one for the return of dirty clothes. That is the whole system. A third cube is how a weekender becomes a bin.

Search packing cubes on Amazon.com

2. Soft bottles, empty until you leave

Decant what you already use. Full-size bottles are how a liquids bag gets pulled. Buy bottles only if you are still carrying glass.

Search travel bottles on Amazon.com

3. A charger that covers the phone and nothing else

One cable, one brick, already tested at home. A “universal” kit with six tips stays in the drawer. If the laptop needs its own charger, pack that one and stop.

Search USB-C chargers on Amazon.com

4. A book light for the person who cannot sleep

Hotel overheads are rude to the person in the other bed. A clip light with a warm bulb solves that. It is not a reading identity. It is a courtesy.

5. A bottle you will refill, not a souvenir flask

Buy one bottle you already like the mouth of. Wide mouth, so you can add ice and wash it. Leave the engraved gift bottles at home.
