Five things that earn a place on a working desk.

This is not a gadget roundup. It is the short list for someone who writes, prints, and ships small digital files from a table that is also the dining table. Buy the item only if the job below is actually yours.

Disclosure: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. Links are Amazon.com searches with tag tips33-20. Prices change. I do not quote them. Nothing here is sold by Club Digital except where a Payhip link says so.

1. A lamp that does not sit in your eyes

Overhead room light makes a laptop glare and makes paper look grey. A warm, directional desk lamp pointed at the page — not at your face — is the one lighting change that matters. Skip RGB bars.

Search warm desk lamps on Amazon.com

2. Paper you can write a price on

A refillable notebook beats another app for the number you are willing to charge. One pen, one page per offer: who it is for, what they download, what it costs. If you will not write it, you will not sell it.

Search dotted notebooks on Amazon.com

3. A timer that is not your phone

The phone is where the draft goes to die. A kitchen timer on the desk is for a 25-minute writing block and nothing else. The same object a host uses for a game night. Different job, same reason: the screen stays face down.

Search kitchen timers on Amazon.com

4. Card stock for anything you call a product

If you sell a printable, print one copy yourself before you take money. Copy paper hides bad margins and bad type. White card stock shows whether the page still works after one handling.

Search card stock on Amazon.com

5. A short power strip, not a new computer

Most “I need a better setup” problems are a laptop, a lamp, and a phone fighting over one wall socket. A short strip with spaced outlets fixes that. Do not buy a docking station until two devices are actually failing.

Search short power strips on Amazon.com
