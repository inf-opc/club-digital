1. A thermometer, not a bigger bird

Dry poultry is a temperature mistake. An instant-read thermometer in the thigh, not touching bone, beats guessing from the color of the skin. Buy one you can wash. Skip the gadget that needs an app at the table.

Search instant-read thermometers on Amazon.com

2. A pan that fits the oven you have

Measure the rack before you order a “turkey roaster.” A pan that does not slide in is not a backup plan on Wednesday night. Light metal shows browning. Very dark pans run hot.

Search roasting pans on Amazon.com

3. Heavy foil and a rest

You need foil to tent the bird while it rests, and a board or platter big enough that the juice does not run onto the counter. Resting is the step people skip when guests are early.

Search heavy-duty foil on Amazon.com

4. One extra pie dish

Someone will bring a pie and someone will bake one. Two dishes. Not a new mixer. A glass or ceramic dish you can see through.

Search pie dishes on Amazon.com

5. Towels for hands, not a new centerpiece

Six extra guests means the one kitchen towel is wet by noon. A stack of cotton towels costs less drama than a floral arrangement. Wash them once before the day so they are not stiff.

Search kitchen towels on Amazon.com
