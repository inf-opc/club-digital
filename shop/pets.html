1. A bandana, not a full suit

If you want a photo, use a bandana or a cape that leaves the eyes, mouth, ears and legs free. Anything that slips over the eyes comes off.

2. A collar you can see in the dark

Trick-or-treat traffic is the actual risk. A reflective collar or a small light that clips to an existing collar beats a plush pumpkin the dog cannot see out of. For a cat, breakaway only.

3. Treats you already know they tolerate

Do not introduce a new protein on a night when the house is full of chocolate and xylitol. Buy more of the usual treat, not a seasonal mystery jar.
