House rules for a night with guests.

Print one copy. Stick it inside a cupboard, not on the front door. Cross out any line you do not mean.

  • Shoes: off / on. Coats go on the chair by the door.
  • Wifi name and password, written here: _______________________________
  • One bathroom. If the door is shut, it is occupied.
  • Photos of people only if they are in the picture and they nodded.
  • Passing is allowed. Nobody performs.
  • Smoking: outside, not the balcony above a neighbour.
  • Last train / taxi time, written here: _______________________________
  • The kitchen closes when the host stands up. Leftovers are offered, not packed in silence.
