When the clocks go back in 2026.

Europe does it first. The United States and Canada do it a week later. Phones usually update alone. Ovens, cars, and one wristwatch do not.

Do these, once

  • Check one oven, one microwave, and the clock in the car.
  • Move one analog watch. Leave the phone alone if it is set to the network.
  • If a medicine is timed to the clock, follow the prescriber. This page is not a dosing schedule.
  • Do not “correct” the same device twice in the morning. The lost hour is the point.

The night after the change gets dark earlier. That is a lighting problem, not a new hobby. A lamp for a working desk.
