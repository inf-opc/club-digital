CLUB DIGITAL / CONTENT TOOLS

30 days. One clear offer.

A practical prompt deck and editable planning sheet for small shops and digital sellers. Choose one product, work through the ideas in any order, and replace every bracketed placeholder with facts from your own business. No promise of views or sales.

Your notes stay in this open page only. Export before closing or reloading. No data is sent anywhere.

DAY 01

Introduce your product

Show the product and name the problem it solves.

Caption starter
Meet [product]: made for [audience] who want [benefit].

DAY 02

Show one use case

Demonstrate one task from start to finish.

Caption starter
