How to use: open this file in your browser. Enter unit costs and fees from your agreement. The result is a contribution per sale before income tax and costs not entered; it is not net accounting profit.

Formulas: contribution = price × (1 − discount) × (1 − fee %) − costs − fixed fees. List price for target margin = (costs + fixed fees) ÷ [(1 − discount) × (1 − fee % − target margin)].
